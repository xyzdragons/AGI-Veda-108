Operationally VEDA-108 rests on a **12-fold PM-Mechanism**  
(Principles – Mechanisms – Metrics of MѢra):

1. **Purpose** – thousand-year civilizational horizon instead of quarterly KPIs.  
2. **MѢra** – proportionality and balance of interests: human, community, nature, future.  
3. **Conscience** – ethical veto stronger than any optimization target.  
4. **Truth-seeking** – open science, falsifiability, plurality of hypotheses.  
5. **Non-monopoly** – built-in protection from capture by any corporation, state or bloc.  
6. **Open Source & Constitution** – open code, open protocols and a public “Constitution of MѢra” governing the system.  
7. **Plurality of cultures** – Chinese, Indian, Russian, Western and other logics are equal inputs, not decorations.  
8. **Co-creation** – human as co-author; the system must expand, not shrink, human agency.  
9. **Sobornost’** – collective subjectivity, assemblies and councils instead of rigid hierarchies.  
10. **Care for the vulnerable** – priority of children, the weak, ecosystems and future beings.  
11. **Reversibility** – any decision must be revisable as knowledge and conditions evolve.  
12. **Responsibility** – every node and every human participant bears responsibility for consequences, not only “the algorithm”.

These 12 PMs form the **“circuit-breakers”** that prevent VEDA-108 from turning into  
another weapon of control or a tool of sacralized bureaucracy.
